Privacy policy

This notice describes the owner-operated desktop tool used by Herman Boma to manage the Machinery in Red YouTube channel.

Information accessed

With the owner’s permission, the app accesses YouTube channel and video metadata, statistics, aggregated analytics and reporting resources. Google OAuth supplies access and refresh tokens. The app does not request the owner’s Google password, Gmail or Google Drive data.

Purpose and permissions

Information is used to measure channel performance, prepare reports and improve future videos. The approved YouTube management scope can support video, playlist, caption and comment operations. The current client implements data collection; additional management features must be implemented and authorized before use.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Analytics snapshots and operating notes are saved in the owner’s local project workspace. The owner’s configured AI assistant may process selected channel information to prepare analysis and channel-management work. Credentials must not be included in assistant messages, published files or reports.

Sharing

The tool uses YouTube API Services and communicates with Google’s APIs and runs within the owner’s chosen computing services and OpenAI Codex assistant. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Reports are for the channel owner; public disclosure requires the owner’s direction.

Retention and deletion

Authorized analytics and statistics are retained while needed for channel analysis and while access remains authorized. Other cached API data must be refreshed or removed within 30 days. Authorization and resource availability are checked regularly. Contact the operator to request deletion; requests will be fulfilled within seven days. Revoking access stops future authorized API requests and triggers review and removal of stored authorized data under YouTube’s required timelines. Removing local data does not delete videos or other data held by YouTube.

Control and revocation

The owner can review and revoke access through Google Account connections. Google’s Privacy Policy also applies to Google’s processing.

Website hosting

These public information pages are hosted by Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes connection information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the site. These pages have no sign-in form, analytics script, advertising or embedded video. Following external links is subject to the destination’s privacy practices.

Contact

For support, privacy questions or deletion requests, email hboma@maverickquantgroup.com.
